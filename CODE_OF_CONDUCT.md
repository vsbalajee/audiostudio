# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors and users regardless of background or experience. Be respectful, focus on the technical issue, offer constructive feedback, and respect others' creative work and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, spam, and uploading private audio without consent are not acceptable.

Maintainers may hide or remove abusive contributions and limit participation to protect the community. If you have a concern, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub. Do not post sensitive details publicly.
